Eritrea
Grades 5–6 · San Francisco
Anchoring Phenomenon
Satellite images showing Eritrean forest loss over 40 years. "Why is this disappearing?"
What Drives the Sequence
Students' remaining questions after each investigation — teacher does not pre-script the path
Justice Move
Student raises: "Who took the resources first?" — colonization named as both science and civic root cause
Whose Knowledge?
Afar pastoralist land management practices cited as expert ecological knowledge; family farming histories as data
🐋 Community-Science Storyline
Orca Sound
Pollution
Grades 4–5 · Pacific Northwest
Anchoring Phenomenon
Video of an orca circling in distress near container ships. "Why is she behaving this way?"
What Drives the Sequence
13 lessons across 7 lesson plans — each step driven by students' unanswered questions from previous investigations
Justice Move
Stage 5 = real letters to Port of Seattle; Lummi Nation's relationship to orca cited as sovereign knowledge, not background
Whose Knowledge?
Student data + scientific research + Indigenous fishing knowledge + shipping policy documents — all equal sources
🌾 Family & Community Storyline (LiP)
Seasonal
Change
K–3 Mixed · Learning in Places
Anchoring Phenomenon
What seasonal changes do families in our community notice? Unit begins with family interviews — not a text or video
What Drives the Sequence
Family phenology observations, seasonal stories, and grandmother knowledge — community expertise organizes lessons
Justice Move
Family knowledge IS the primary data — not supplementary context. Community members present as scientific authorities

Definition: What Is a Storyline?
"A coherent sequence of lessons in which each step is driven by students' noticings, wonderings, and questions that arise from phenomena AND from their families and communities."
— Adapted from Next Gen Storylines / AST
Whole Class Discussion Prompts
Student-Driven
"Which unit feels most student-driven? What would schools need to change for this to be the norm — not the exception?"
Family Phenomena
"The LiP unit begins with FAMILY observations, not a school text. What does that require of the teacher? Of the institution?"
BPQs in Storylines
"Where in these storylines would Back-Pocket Questions be used? Which unit would make BPQs hardest to pre-plan — and why?"
Your Placement
"In your placement school, what phenomenon would be scientifically rigorous AND connected to something families in that community are actually experiencing?"
Justice in Each
"Look at the three 'Justice Moves.' Which one goes deepest? What makes the LiP version different from the Rainforest version?"
Counternarrative
"Each of these units pushes against a mainstream narrative (deforestation is just poverty; orcas are just endangered; seasons are just weather). What is the counternarrative — and why does it matter?"
