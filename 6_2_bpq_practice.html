The BPQ Progression: Observable → Mechanism
WHATObservable Level
Presses students to describe what they notice, observe, or measure. Stays at the surface — important to establish, but not the goal.
"What do you notice about the surface temperature?" · "What happened to the puddle?"
HOWProcess Level
Presses students to explain the process or relationship. Moves from pattern to cause. The transition zone.
"How does that surface change what happens to the sun's energy?" · "How does the sound interfere?"
WHYMechanism Level
Presses students to explain the underlying mechanism — why it works the way it does at a particle/system level.
"Why does a darker material absorb more energy than a lighter one?" · "Why can't the orca locate prey when boats are present?"

Example BPQ Progression for Statement C (Urban Heat Island)
WHAT: "What do you notice about the concrete that makes you say it gets hot?"
HOW: "How does the concrete change what happens to the sun's energy when it lands on the surface?"
WHY: "Why does a material that absorbs more energy end up staying hot even after the sun goes down — what's happening at the level of the particles?"
